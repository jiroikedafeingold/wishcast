We've all done it: checked one weather app, then another, then another, until one finally told us what we wanted to hear. Wishcast skips the hunting. It looks at about 170 forecasts at once and shows you the best case for the weather you're hoping for.

HOW IT WORKS
Wishcast downloads forecasts from 9 global weather models plus every run of 5 ensemble models: about 170 forecasts for each day. For every value (high, low, rain, snow, clouds, wind), it picks the one that best fits your wish. The high can come from one forecast and the low from another, so a Wishcast day is a best case, not a prediction.

It stays honest about it:
- Each day shows how many forecasts actually show its high and low.
- Tap a day to see where every value came from and how it was chosen.
- A Reality Check counts the single forecasts that fully deliver your wish.
- A chart plots every forecast so you can see how far out on a limb your wish is.
- "How Wishcast Works" explains every rule, with credits and references.

EVERYTHING YOU EXPECT FROM A WEATHER APP
- Current conditions, a 7-day outlook and hourly forecasts
- Pick any date up to about six months ahead. Past a week it's labeled a long shot, past two weeks a wild guess.
- Sunrise, sunset, UV index and chance of precipitation
- Saved places, city search and current location
- Home Screen widgets, plus Lock Screen widgets on iPhone
- Layouts for iPhone and iPad

No account and no sign-in. Your location is only used to request forecasts.
